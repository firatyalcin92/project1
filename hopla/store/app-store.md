### Description (EN)

Meet Hopdog, the bravest sausage you'll ever see.

Hold anywhere on the screen to charge, release to hop. The longer you hold, the farther he flies. Land on the
next block, table, chair or TV — and keep going as far as you can.

• One-touch controls: easy to learn, hard to master
• Land dead center for bonus points and chain perfect hops for bigger combos
• A sausage with feelings: he squints when you charge, grins on a perfect landing and sees stars when he falls
• Calm, colorful 3D world with soft shadows
• Plays fully offline — no account, no ads
• Pick a nickname to join the world and country leaderboards (optional)

How far can your sausage hop?
